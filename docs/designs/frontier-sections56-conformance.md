all IR or all mathematical inputs in the draft. The authoritative version is

The generic regional loss certificate is a ceiling, not an exact measurement.
Its walk and payload counts are supplied host-side execution evidence; the
compiler does not solve arbitrary while trip counts. Parent boundary completions
can add cross-visit order even when an imported child's internal contribution is
zero. Opaque callees and proper completion children currently use the coarse
internal ceiling instead of recursively recovering specialized loss evidence.

The stronger periodic engine and its fold adapter do not establish a new
executable repeated-child import. Until that provider supplies upper-realization
equality, the general production fallback retains its coarse bound. Likewise,
no ordering-changing resource repair, arbitrary protocol synthesis, or runtime
dependency evaluator is introduced by this plan.
