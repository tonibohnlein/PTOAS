## Step 13 implementation scope

`OriginalPreparation` freezes source/deadline adjacency and source hooks after
request formation. Physical source adjacency shares cell/role buckets; typed
sources have their own family buckets. This avoids eager source/deadline products
and preserves distinct families, engines and deadlines. The hook retains both
its sufficient analytical cut and any executable enclosing cut, with an explicit
obstruction when these differ. Descriptor references are walked once without
guard valuation expansion. Completion prerequisites also contribute hooks.

Finite provider-declared support roles are validated and visited once. Link
shifts stay on the stored relations; no dynamic visits are unfolded. Missing
roles, invalid intervals, unsupported shift/applicability qualification and stale
identities retain obstructions and conservative hooks. `formed()` reports frozen
formation; `repertoireComplete()` reports whether every promised subscription
has its premises. Neither means every descriptor is usable or grants completion.

The lazy consequence query retains the due and both generating obligations,
requires an exact full invocation D1 interval, and joins the same translated
middle phase. Its applicability is the conjunction of original membership
conditions; simple inconsistent required literals are pruned without valuations.
The whole interval and original obligation identity participate in memoization.
Cross-cell chains are supported. Different loop visits, recurring/D4 middle
matching, nonzero support-shift qualification, and wider interval/selector
variants remain explicit precision limits. No obligation is removed.

Step 13 native tests cover unconditional and conditional cross-cell chains,
opposite branches, repeated visits, query-key distinctions, early hooks,
analytical versus enclosing executable cuts, finite shifted cycles, missing
roles, lazy output, and stale snapshots. Final integrated validation and reviewer
acceptance are recorded below.
